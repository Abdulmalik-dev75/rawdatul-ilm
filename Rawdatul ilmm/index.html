ABOUT US

A place to learn, grow and understand.

Rawdatul Ilm (روضة العلم) is an online Islamic learning program created to help young learners develop a stronger connection with Islamic knowledge.

We focus on essential areas such as Tajweed Qur'an, Hadith and Basic Arabic, taught in a clear and encouraging environment.
